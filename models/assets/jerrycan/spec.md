# jerrycan - reference specification (pipeline test asset)

Wehrmacht-Einheitskanister, 20 L fuel can. Class: small_prop. Budget 500-2,000 triangles (max 3,000). Front axis -Y (broad face toward the viewer).

## Sources (what was actually read)
- Wikipedia "Jerrycan": 1937 design by Müller of Schwelm; pressed steel; **three handles**; large spout with flip-top closure (a hole in the closure retainer takes a securing pin/wire with lead seal); **recessed welded seam** around the "equator"; **cross-like indentations** on the sides; air pocket under the handles; capacity 20 L. US copy differs by simplified "X" indentations and rolled seams.
- International Military Antiques (dealer listings of original cans, dated 1940-1944): approx. **18" H x 14" W x 6.5" D** (about 0.457 x 0.356 x 0.165 m); embossed text **"Kraftstoff 20 L / Feuergefährlich"** plus maker codes and year; "Wehrmacht" waffenamt-style marks; seen painted tropical tan, panzergrau and later olive/dunkelgelb.

Dimensions come from sale listings ("approximate measurements"), not a drawing, so +-1 cm is the honest accuracy.

## Features that must be present
1. Rectangular pressed-steel body with rounded edges; two shell halves joined by a **recessed seam running round the middle of the narrow side** (the "equator").
2. Raised/pressed **cross (X) stiffening** on both broad faces, with a pressed frame; no flat plain panels.
3. **Three-bar handle** along the top (outer grips plus centre), with an open gap under it; air pocket means the top is a raised block.
4. **Spout** near one top corner with **flip-top cap and cam-lever latch** and a retainer ring.
5. Embossed stencil text area on the broad face (texture or relief, not geometry).
6. Closed low-poly collision hull.

## Materials and finish
Painted pressed steel (German can: dunkelgelb or panzergrau, olive on later cans), worn paint with rust at edges, seam, handle and cap; steel dull grey for cap and latch. All image-textured, no flat colours.

## Deliberately omitted (not worth the triangles)
Interior, thread details of the spout, individual weld beads, the rubber gasket.
